Nano Enterprise

Nano Enterprise is a full-featured, intuitive management app designed exclusively for small businesses on macOS. Effortlessly handle purchase orders, sales, suppliers, warehouses, and inventory—without training or internet required. Works offline, everywhere.

Instant Business Snapshot

Get a real-time overview of cash flow, inventory levels, and orders. Stay informed at a glance, no digging required.

Seamless Record-Keeping

Maintain up-to-date bank transactions, track sales, suppliers, manufacturing, and warehouses. Accurate records for clients, products, vendors, and teams—built in.

Powerful Reporting

Generate filtered financial reports, sales analytics, bestsellers, margins, inventory turnover, and stock movement. Export to spreadsheets effortlessly.
